Recipes Boiled Fried Poached Scrambled Plain Omelette

Recipe

Fry it up!

Ingredients

  • Eggs
  • Oil
  • Salt & Pepper

Method

  1. Crack the eggs into a pan of shallow hot oil.
  2. Cook slowly, basting occasionally.
  3. Drain off excess fat and serve
  4. Serve on a suitable dish
  5. Season to taste

Note

  • The white should be set and the yolk still soft.

Equipment List

  • Frying Pan
  • Fish Slice (spatula)
  • Paper Towel
  • Suitable plate
